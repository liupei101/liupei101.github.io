🎉 🎉 🎉 We are thrilled to release our single-cell foundation model **CellNet**: [Panoramic modelling of single-cell transcriptomes at scale with CellNet](cellnet.top). 🔥 CellNet enables **panoramic modelling of single-cell transcriptomes** by integrating DNA-derived gene identities and measured RNA expression into **a shared image representation**. 🧬 We have made our 231M single-cell transcriptome image atlas from 40 species publicly available to facilitate future research. 🌏 Please visit our CellNet website for details: [cellnet.top](cellnet.top). 🍻 Let's build virtual cell together! 👏
