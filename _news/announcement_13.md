:fire: Two co-authored papers, 
**DPsurv** (Dual-Prototype Evidential Fusion for Uncertainty-Aware and Interpretable Whole Slide Image Survival Prediction) and **CAML** (A Conflict-Aware Molecular Language Model Merging Framework for Multi-Constraint Molecular Generation), are accepted by [ICML 2026](https://openreview.net/group?id=ICML.cc/2026/Conference) and [ACL 2026](https://openreview.net/group?id=aclweb.org/ACL/2026/Conference). Congratulations to Yucheng and Xuanbai.
